## The Best Apps Like Angie's List for Finding Local Pros

If you just want to hire someone — fix a leaky roof, rewire a kitchen, shoot a wedding — these are the platforms that consistently come up as Angi alternatives.

**Thumbtack.** Probably the most direct competitor. Pros pay per quote they send, and customers get matched with several pros at once. The interface is cleaner than Angi's, and the profile detail tends to be deeper. It works well for one-off jobs but gets expensive for pros who bid on a lot of projects.

**HomeAdvisor.** Now part of the same parent company as Angi, so the lines blur. It leans heavily toward home-improvement leads. Pros pay for leads whether or not they win the job, which is the same complaint people have about Angi.

**Houzz Pro.** Strong for design and remodeling. Less of a pure lead-gen platform, more of a marketing suite for design-build professionals. Good if you're remodeling, not great if you need a dishwasher repair.

**Yelp.** Still a default for many consumers, though reviews skew toward restaurants and retail. The "Yelp for Pros" use case works but feels like a side effect rather than the core product.

**Porch.** Another home-services-focused option, with a model that pushes pros into paid memberships. Useful but limited to home improvement.

Each of these solves a narrow version of the Angie's List problem. None of them let you actually *own* the directory — which is the gap that brings us to the next section.
